- The CLI uses match_query_clip_live().

- Use 64x64 grayscale frames or another clearly documented low resolution.
- Use diagonal NCC or patch-based NCC for fast filtering.
- Avoid full 256x256 NCC during the database-wide coarse scan.
- Keep full 256x256 NCC for final fine verification.
- Keep brightness robustness by using normalized, zero-mean comparison.
